---
title: Request inspector
description: See every request your code made, which scenario answered it, and what streamed back.
---

Open a mock's **Requests** tab to see its traffic live.

Each request shows:

- the endpoint format, model and client SDK
- the normalised messages, tools and settings
- every scenario above the match, with each condition marked pass or fail
- the matched scenario and its rendered reply
- every chunk, with its time offset
- usage, cost and total duration

## Filters

Filter by scenario, status, conversation id, header or text in the prompt. **Unmatched** lists requests that hit no scenario, which is the fastest way to find prompts you forgot to mock.

## From a request to a scenario

**Create scenario** on any request pre-fills a scenario that matches its prompt, so a surprise request becomes a mocked one in two clicks.

## Locally

The local server serves the same inspector at `http://localhost:4010/_inspect`, and `loremllm logs <mock>` tails it in the terminal.

## Retention

Requests are kept for 7 days on the free plan and 30 days on team plans.
